How to use: Current revenue and cost of goods sold come from QuickBooks and are locked. Everything else is yours to change. Move the revenue and COGS sliders for Scenario and Target, and edit any expense line. QuickBooks only gives one total for operating expenses, so split that total into the lines below it. Keep the total the same for Current.
Plan: the Target revenue starts at $500K a month ($6M a year) and the plan margin is 18-30% (from your July plan). Both are only starting points.
Heads up: QuickBooks was edited between Oct 19 and Oct 22 and income and cost of goods sold moved by $17-19K. Treat Current as approximate until the books are final. This is net operating profit. It leaves out other income and expense.
